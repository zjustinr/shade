# App Store listing — Cool Corners

Ready-to-paste App Store Connect copy, grounded in the October 2026
competitive research (see the "Shade and cooling app landscape" report).
The findings this listing is built on:

- The "shade / shaded walk / cool walk" keyword family is effectively
  unguarded (the strongest incumbent has 6 ratings); "sun position" and
  "sun tracker" are contested by six 4.7★+ apps — stay off them.
- The best-known shade app's top review complaint is its paywalled
  time-of-day control, so "free — no ads, no accounts" belongs in the
  first lines, and the time slider stays free forever.
- No competitor localizes its LISTING in Traditional Chinese or
  Vietnamese; doing so is how the app surfaces in those users' stores.
- Only one shade app on earth works offline; say it prominently.
- Discovery in this category is heat-wave-press-driven, not search-driven:
  the listing supports the story, it doesn't replace outreach.

**Translation gate (§8):** the zh-Hant and vi copy below is a first-pass
draft and must be reviewed by the community translator before it is pasted
into App Store Connect, exactly like in-app strings.

**§0 guard:** nothing in this listing may present the app as a directory
of cooling centers. The City's map is referenced as the City's service,
one tap away. Keep it that way in every future edit.

## English (en-US)

- **Name** (≤30): `Cool Corners: Chinatown Shade`
- **Subtitle** (≤30): `Shaded walking routes, Boston`
- **Keywords** (≤100, don't repeat name/subtitle words):
  `shady,walk,cool,heat,heatwave,sun,sunny,map,route,summer,winter,warm,elder,senior`
- **Promotional text** (≤170, editable without review — swap seasonally):
  - Summer: `Heat advisory? See which side of the street is shaded right now, and take the cooler way there.`
  - Winter: `Cold snap? The same map finds the sunniest walk instead.`

**Description:**

```
The City's map shows you where to cool off. Cool Corners shows you the
coolest way to walk there.

Boston's Chinatown can differ by tens of degrees between the sunny and
shaded sides of the same street. Cool Corners maps where shade actually
is — building by building, tree by tree, half-hour by half-hour — and
plans walking routes that stay in it.

• Shade map for any time of day, any season
• Walking routes compared by % in shade, distance, and time — pick
  most shade, shortest, or in between
• In winter it flips: find the sunniest, warmest walk instead
• Everyday destinations: pharmacies, the library, clinics, parks
• Works offline — the map, the shade model and the route planner are
  all on your phone
• English, 繁體中文, Tiếng Việt
• Free. No ads, no accounts, no tracking — nothing is collected, ever.

Honest by design: shade is modelled from City of Boston building and
tree data, and checked against on-the-street temperature readings taken
by a Chinatown youth crew with infrared thermometers. Where the model
and the measurements disagree, the map shows both. Awnings, scaffolding
and shelters are not modelled — check the street before you rely on it.

The City of Boston's official cooling-resources map is one tap away
inside the app.

Built with and for the Chinatown community.
```
